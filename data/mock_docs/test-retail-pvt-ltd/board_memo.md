## Q1 2025 review
Revenue in March 2025 was 82,97,362 INR, compared with 1,30,58,964 INR in February 2025; revenue fell by 47,61,602 INR (36.5%). The main cause was the temporary closure of several of our largest mall stores in Maharashtra for most of March 2025, after the mall operators ordered a fire-safety re-inspection of older buildings. Footfall in the remaining stores was normal, but the closed stores carry a large share of apparel sales.

Management also postponed the spring end-of-season sale from March to April 2025 so that it would run when all stores were open again. This moved some demand out of March.

## Impact on profit
Store rent, salaries and electricity continued during the closures, so expenses rose by 1,79,690 INR (1.7%) from February 2025 to March 2025 while revenue fell. Profit in March 2025 was -23,56,941 INR, the only loss-making month in the period.

## Recovery
All stores reopened in the first week of April 2025 and the postponed sale ran in April. Revenue rose by 30,74,889 INR (37.1%) from March 2025 to April 2025 and rose by 15,66,967 INR (13.8%) from April 2025 to May 2025. The board considers the March 2025 drop a one-off event, not a change in customer demand.

## Decisions
The board approved a fire-safety audit of every store before the next festive season and asked management to negotiate rent relief clauses for forced closures in future lease renewals.
