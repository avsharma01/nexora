## Key risks
1. Customer concentration. A small group of large customers contributes a large share of revenue; losing one would be visible in monthly revenue.
2. Currency. Some customers are billed in US dollars, so rupee movements change reported revenue.
3. Renewal timing. Annual plans renew in batches, so monthly revenue can move with renewal dates rather than demand.
4. Hiring discipline. Restarted hiring could push expenses up faster than revenue.

## Risks we do not see in the KPIs
